## Limitations

- Uses only past box-score stats. It does not know about offseason trades, depth-chart changes, coaching changes, or rookies; the LLM's web search covers those.
- Trained only on players who played at least 4 games in both seasons, so it predicts how a player scores *when he plays*, not whether he stays healthy.
