## Limitations

- Kicker scoring swings a lot from year to year: it depends on how often the offense stalls in range, which barely carries over. Expect only a modest gain over the league average.
- Trained on kickers with at least 4 games in both seasons, so it predicts scoring *when he kicks*, not whether he keeps the job.
- Team context (offense, next season's schedule, indoor games) is the kicker's season-t team. A kicker who changes teams is projected as if he hadn't; the LLM's web search covers signings and cuts.
