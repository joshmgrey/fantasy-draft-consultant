## Limitations

- D/ST output swings a lot from year to year (turnovers and return TDs are largely luck), so even a good model is only modestly better than the league average.
- The model mostly improves *how many* points it expects (it pulls extreme seasons back toward the average); it ranks teams about as well as last season's finish does, no better. Use it as a sanity check on a D/ST's price, not as a ranking.
- Uses past team box scores and next season's schedule only. It does not know about free agency, the draft, coaching changes, or injuries; the LLM's web search covers those.
- Points allowed is the opponent's final score, including points the offense gave up (pick-sixes, fumble returns). Some leagues exclude those.
- 32 teams a season means every test score rests on 32 predictions.
